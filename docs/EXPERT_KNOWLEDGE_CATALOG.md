# IQ200 Expert Knowledge Catalog

This catalog is a curated, versioned set of evidence-first trading principles for Binance-oriented crypto trading. It is deliberately conservative: it describes conditions and failure conditions and never promises profit.

## Governance

- Catalog entries are source-category tagged and versioned.
- A knowledge item is not a trading strategy by itself.
- Strategies must pass backtest, out-of-sample, cost/slippage and Testnet gates before production consideration.
- New lessons remain quarantined until independently validated.
- Current market/news data always outranks stale static knowledge when the two conflict.
- Missing or contradictory evidence defaults to WAIT.

## Coverage

Risk, market structure, momentum, volume, market regime, news and macro risk, historical analysis, backtesting, exchange execution, portfolio risk, behavioral risk, multi-agent decision making, model calibration, learning governance and safety.

## Limitation

No finite catalog can contain all trading knowledge, and no catalog can guarantee profitable trades. The catalog is intended to make reasoning explicit, testable and auditable rather than to replace live evidence.
